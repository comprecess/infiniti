INFINITI Network Model
Strategic Vision for the Next Evolution of INFINITI
Version: 1.0 Status: Strategic Vision (Not MVP Scope)

Executive Summary
INFINITI is evolving beyond Talent-as-a-Service, Outstaffing and Growth & Exit.
The long-term objective is to build a self-reinforcing ecosystem where:
Founders
Experts & Advisors
Investors
Buyers
interact around one common goal:
Increasing the value of businesses.
The platform should ultimately function as a Growth & Exit Operating System where participants help each other create, finance, scale and exit companies.

Core Principle
Most platforms focus on:
jobs;
talent matching;
fundraising;
acquisitions.
INFINITI focuses on:
Value Creation.
Everything inside the platform should contribute to increasing company value.

Why Experts Matter More Than Capital
Most founders initially believe they need investors.
In reality they often need:
better product leadership;
better sales execution;
stronger positioning;
operational improvements.
The platform should prioritize:
Growth Experts
before
Capital Providers.
Capital accelerates growth.
Experts create growth.
