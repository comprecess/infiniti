## Growth & Exit Program (M&A Preparation)

The Growth & Exit Program helps Founders digitize their business, understand its real value, and increase that value before a sale or investment round. This is a structured engagement managed by a Deal Manager (INFINITI team member) on behalf of the Founder.

### Key Modules:

**1. Onboarding (Exit Audit)**
Collection of baseline financial metrics (MRR, Revenue, EBITDA, Margin) and company information. This data feeds into the Valuation Engine and helps the Deal Manager understand the current state of the business.

**2. Deal Room (Virtual Data Room)**
A secure document repository with 8 standard folders for Due Diligence:
- Financial Documents
- Legal Documents
- HR & Team
- Technical Documentation
- Operations
- Commercial & Sales
- Compliance & Regulatory
- Strategic Documents

Founders upload documents. Investors and Buyers can view (read-only) documents shared with them.

**3. Valuation Engine**
Calculates the company's value using a base metric and an industry multiplier:
- Current Value = Base Metric × Current Multiplier (what the company is worth today)
- Projected Value = Base Metric × Projected Multiplier (expected value after Growth Plan execution)
- Best Case Value = Base Metric × Best Case Multiplier (maximum achievable value)

**4. Growth Plan**
A Kanban-style board of initiatives (Growth Items) designed to increase the company's value. Each Growth Item has:
- Category (compliance, commercial, technical, operational)
- Implementation cost
- Impact on the valuation multiplier
- Priority level

### Approval Flow (Business Transaction Logic)
When a Founder approves a Growth Item:
1. The item status changes to "approved"
2. A Task is created for the INFINITI team
3. A commercial Offer (Quote) is generated automatically
4. An Invoice is generated for payment

After payment, the INFINITI team executes the initiative.

## Role-Based Access Model

The platform enforces strict role-based access for Exit Deal projects:

**Deal Manager (Admin):** Full access. Creates projects, sets up valuations, proposes Growth Items, invites external participants, manages the entire deal lifecycle.

**Founder (Owner):** Full access to their own project. Completes onboarding, uploads documents to the Deal Room, views all valuation details, approves Growth Items, and pays invoices.

**Investor:** Read-only access to the Deal Room, summary valuation (no calculation details), and read-only view of the Growth Plan. Cannot see onboarding data or approve items.

**Buyer:** Most restricted access. Read-only Deal Room, summary current valuation only. Cannot see the Growth Plan, projected values, or onboarding data.
